Viral / Hook Reel
Goal → Max Reach & New Audience

  • "Chennai la cheap land iruka — yes!"
  • "Middle class ku best investment 2025"
  • "5 lakh la land — miss pannadha"
  • "Salary-la plot vaanga mudiyuma? Yes!"

⚡ EVERY REEL MUST FOLLOW THIS FORMULA

Step 01
HOOK
⏱ First 3 seconds — MOST IMPORTANT
  • "10 lakhs la plot venuma?"
  • "Idha miss pannina regret pannuveenga"
  • "Chennai la cheap land iruka?"
  • Must create curiosity or shock
  • Text on screen + voice together
Step 02
BODY
⏱ 4–45 seconds — Deliver Value
  • Show exact location on map
  • Clear price mention
  • 3 key benefits (road, EB, DTCP)
  • Drone or walkthrough footage
  • Keep it under 60 sec total
Step 03
CTA
⏱ Last 5 seconds — Force Action
  • "DM the word PLOT"
  • "WhatsApp link bio-la iruku"
  • "Call pannunga — 24hrs reply"
  • Show your number on screen
  • One action only — don't confuse

Ads Strategy

When to run, how much to spend, and how to scale

Phase 1
Organic Testing

Post your reel organically first. Wait 2–3 days. Let Instagram push it naturally. This tells you if the content is strong BEFORE you spend money.

A reel is a WINNER if it gets:
  • ✓ 5,000+ views organically
  • ✓ High saves and shares (not just likes)
  • ✓ Comments asking "price?" or "location?"
  • ✓ DMs and WhatsApp messages received
❌ If the reel gets less than 1K views — don't run ads on it. Make a new one.
Phase 2
Paid Scaling

Take the winning reel to Meta Ads Manager. Run it as a paid ad targeting the right audience.

Testing Budget₹300/day × 5–7 days
Scaling Budget₹500/day × 10 days
Winner Budget₹1000/day × ongoing
Target Audience: Age 25–45 · Tamil Nadu · Interests: Real estate, investment, home buying · Languages: Tamil + English

📅 ADS TIMELINE — DAY BY DAY (FROM DAY 15)

DAY 15–17
Launch Ad
₹300/day. Test winner reel. Don't touch the ad for 3 days — let algorithm learn.
DAY 18–20
Check Results
Is CPL (cost per lead) under ₹150? Good. Above ₹300? Stop, test new creative.
DAY 21–25
Scale Budget
If winning — increase to ₹500/day. Don't double in one shot. Increase by 30% max.
DAY 26–30
Best Ad Only
₹1000/day on the single best creative. All leads → WhatsApp → call → site visit.

Mistakes to Avoid

These will kill your growth — avoid every single one

✕
Random Posting
Posting whenever you feel like it. Instagram rewards consistency. Miss 3 days = algorithm drops you.
✕
100% Sales Content
Every post saying "buy this" feels like spam. Mix education (40%) + showcase (40%) + viral (20%).
✕
No CTA in Reels
People like the video but don't know what to do next. Always end with ONE clear action.
✕
Running Ads on Weak Videos
If a reel gets less than 1K organic views, no amount of money will fix it. Test first, always.
✕
Slow DM Replies
Replying after 2–3 hours = cold lead. Leads are hottest in the first 10 minutes. Reply instantly.
✕
Poor Hook
If the first 3 seconds are boring, nobody watches. Your hook is 70% of your reel's success.
